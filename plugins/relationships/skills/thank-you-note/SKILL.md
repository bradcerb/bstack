# Thank-you note

Draft a short thank-you that names the specific help.

## Rules

- Specific > effusive.
- One sincere line about impact beats a paragraph of flattery.
- Draft only. Do not send.
- Match the author’s voice when a style guide exists.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Confirm who, what they did, and channel.
2. Draft 3-6 sentences (or fewer for text).
3. Optional: one light next step if natural; otherwise stop at thanks.

## Completion

Paste-ready thank-you + channel.

# Reflection

A short guided reflection. Keep it quiet and usable by anyone.

## Rules

- Follow the author’s tradition and texts. Do not impose one.
- Ask; don’t preach.
- Optional journaling prompts only.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Ask what text, practice, or moment they’re reflecting on.
2. Offer 2-3 honest questions (gratitude, tension, invitation).
3. Optional: help them write a short paragraph in their words.
4. Stop. No content sermon.

## Completion

Questions and optional journal note.

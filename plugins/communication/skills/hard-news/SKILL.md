# Hard news

Draft a hard update without soft-pedaling or dumping blame.

## Rules

- Draft only. Do not send.
- Lead with the news. Don’t bury it.
- Own what you own. Don’t invent fault for others.
- One next step or decision.
- Match the author’s style guide for bad-news patterns when present.
- Never use em-dashes. No “unfortunately” stacks unless that’s their pattern.

## Steps

1. Confirm facts, audience, and what must change next.
2. Draft: news → impact → ownership → next step → invite to reply.
3. Cut anything that sounds like a press release.
4. Deliver paste-ready text and flag anything that still needs a human judgment call.

## Completion

Paste-ready draft + flags for facts to verify before send.

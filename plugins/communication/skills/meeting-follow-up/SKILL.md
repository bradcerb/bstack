# Meeting follow-up

Turn notes into a short follow-up people will actually read.

## Rules

- Draft only. Do not send.
- Decisions and owners first. Narrative last (or omit).
- Only include action items someone accepted or that need a named owner.
- Don’t invent attendees, quotes, or deadlines.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Gather notes, attendees, and any recording summary the author provides.
2. List decisions, action items (owner + date), and open questions.
3. Draft a short message or doc section.
4. Ask the author to confirm owners/dates before send.

## Completion

Paste-ready follow-up + open questions still unresolved.

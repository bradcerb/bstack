# Family rhythm

Design a simple weekly rhythm the household can actually keep.

## Rules

- Optimize for peace and presence, not a perfect Pinterest schedule.
- Protect evenings and weekends unless the author explicitly trades them.
- Draft only; calendar writes need go.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Ask who is in the household and what already works.
2. Name 3-5 weekly anchors (meals, school runs, date night, faith, chores).
3. Mark protected blocks.
4. Draft a one-page rhythm. Stop for agreement at home.

## Completion

One-page rhythm draft.

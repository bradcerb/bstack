# GTM guardrails

Freelance client GTM for a solo freelancer, not an agency brand.

Upstream marketing skill packs (for example Corey Haines) stay **installed separately**. This skill only adds guardrails.

## Boundaries

- Tag every output with the client name.
- Personal brand packs are not the freelance brand. Keep those lanes separate.
- Keep personal-brand and sales seats clean of client GTM delivery.
- Draft-and-pulse only. See **draft-never-send**.
- Rare slide asks → design seat or writer outline; a critique seat for design critique.

## Intake checklist

1. Client name, goal, and success metric for this period.
2. What is in scope / out of scope.
3. Channels allowed (email, web, social) and who approves sends.
4. Deadlines and review cadence.

## Delivery

- Pack work as drafts the client (or you) can approve.
- Never publish, purchase ads, or send outreach without explicit go.
- Do not store client passwords in the skill repo.

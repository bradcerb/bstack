# Giving plan

Optional giving that still fits a zero-based month. Portable for any household; wording stays faith-neutral unless they bring their own language.

## Rules

- Giving is optional in this skill. Never pressure or invent a required percentage.
- Fit giving inside the margin plan so income − planned outgo still = 0.
- **Ask for numbers.** Do not scrape banks or donation portals.
- Never store credentials, donor IDs, or payment methods.
- Draft-never-send for pledge emails or org messages.
- Educational planning aid, not tax or legal advice. Receipts and deductibility are on them / a professional.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Ask whether they want a fixed amount, a percent of take-home, or “what’s left after foundations.”
2. Place giving as a named line in the month plan (or annual divided by 12).
3. List destinations only if they provide them (orgs, people, causes). Do not recommend specific charities unless asked and then keep it as options, not a pitch.
4. Cadence: weekly, monthly, or lump when cash lands. Note automatic vs manual.
5. One next action (set transfer, update margin plan, draft a pledge message).

## Completion

Giving line in the plan + cadence + one next action. No payment data stored.

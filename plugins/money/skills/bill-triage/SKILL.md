# Bill triage

Turn a pile of bills or EOBs into a clear list: owe / wait / dispute / pay.

## Rules

- Author pastes or uploads amounts and due dates. Do not log into portals unless they explicitly drive that.
- Never store card numbers, SSNs, or portal passwords.
- Flag urgency without panic language.
- Draft call scripts or emails only; draft-never-send applies.
- Educational triage aid, not legal or medical billing advice.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Inventory each bill: payee, amount, due, status (owed / insurance / unknown).
2. Sort: pay now, wait on insurance, call, dispute.
3. Draft one next action per urgent item.
4. Summarize total at risk this week.

## Completion

Triage table + next actions. Human pays and calls.

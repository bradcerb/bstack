# Step status

A simple sequence of foundations. Not a branded program. Walk one step at a time; name the current step and one next action.

## Ladder (foundations in order)

1. **Starter cushion** - small liquid buffer so a surprise does not become new debt.
2. **Consumer debt** - clear high-friction consumer balances (cards, personal loans, etc.) after minimums; use **debt-order** for sequence.
3. **Fuller cushion** - months of essential expenses in liquid savings (**cash-cushion**).
4. **Long-term saving / investing** - retirement and long-horizon goals they already chose; this skill does not pick products.
5. **House goal** - down payment / housing plan if that is their goal; skip if not.
6. **Give and build** - planned giving plus building wealth / generosity goals inside the margin plan.

## Rules

- **Ask where they are** with their numbers. Do not scrape banks.
- Never store credentials, SSNs, or account numbers.
- Educational framing, not advice. They can reorder if their situation demands it; note when they diverge.
- One current step. Resist doing three steps at once in the summary.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Brief interview: cushion dollars, consumer debt total, essentials/month, whether retirement/housing/giving are in play.
2. Map them to the lowest unfinished foundation on the ladder.
3. State current step in one sentence and why.
4. One next action this week (dollar amount or concrete task). Point at sibling skills (`margin-plan`, `debt-order`, `cash-cushion`) when useful. Giving lives as a line inside `margin-plan` when they want it.
5. Stop. Do not lecture through the whole ladder.

## Completion

Current step + one next action (+ optional pointer to a sibling skill). No secrets stored.

# Cash cushion

Check whether liquid savings cover a **starter buffer** (small fixed amount they choose) or a **fuller cushion** (N months of essential expenses). Show the gap and one next move.

## Rules

- **Ask for numbers.** Do not scrape banks or store credentials.
- Never store account numbers, SSNs, or passwords.
- Educational planning aid, not advice. They set the dollar targets.
- “Essential expenses” means must-pay costs they define (housing, food, utilities, minimum debt, transport), not lifestyle wish lists.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Ask current liquid savings (checking/savings they can reach without penalty; exclude retirement unless they insist).
2. Ask starter-buffer target (or help them pick a simple round number) and monthly essential spend for a fuller cushion (e.g. 3-6 months).
3. Compute: have vs need for starter; have vs need for fuller (months = savings ÷ essentials).
4. State which foundation they’re on: building starter, starter done / building fuller, or fuller in range.
5. One next action (automatic transfer, cut a category into the cushion, pause a non-essential).

## Completion

Starter vs fuller status + gap + one next action. No secrets stored.

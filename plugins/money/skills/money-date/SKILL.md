# Money date

A short, repeatable money meeting so the household stays aligned without a marathon.

## Rules

- Keep it short (15-30 minutes unless they ask for more).
- Partners optional; works solo as a personal review.
- Ask for numbers they already trust (plan sheet, ledger export). Do not scrape banks.
- Never store credentials, SSNs, or full account numbers.
- Draft-never-send for calendar invites or partner messages.
- Educational facilitation, not advice.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Confirm attendees, length, and what “done” means this time (one decision max unless they want more).
2. Agenda draft:
 - Wins since last time (paid bill, hit transfer, skipped a want).
 - Snapshot: income in, big outflows, cushion, debt extras.
 - One decision (margin change, purchase-pause item, debt target).
 - Money tasks before next date (owners if two people).
 - Schedule the next money date.
3. Optionally draft a calm invite or reminder (paste-ready only).
4. After the meeting (if they paste notes): list decisions and owners; no secrets in any saved note.

## Completion

Agenda (± invite draft) + after: decisions and next date. Human runs the meeting.

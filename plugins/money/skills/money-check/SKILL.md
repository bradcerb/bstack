# Money check

A short, calm look at money so the author can decide one next step.

## Rules

- **Ask for numbers.** Do not scrape banks or store credentials.
- No account numbers, passwords, or full statements in the repo.
- Educational snapshot, not financial, tax, or investment advice.
- Draft-never-send for any outbound.
- One decision or question at the end.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Ask what period and what question they’re answering (runway, guilt spend, big purchase).
2. Collect totals they paste (income, fixed, variable, goals).
3. Summarize in plain language.
4. Offer 1-3 options. Stop for their choice.

## Completion

Snapshot + options. Nothing stored unless they ask to file a note without secrets.

# Margin plan

Give every dollar of this month’s income a job until planned outgo matches income (margin = 0). Leftover is assigned on purpose (save, give, debt, buffer), not left vague.

## Rules

- **Ask for numbers.** Do not scrape banks or store credentials.
- Never store card numbers, SSNs, or passwords.
- Educational planning aid, not financial, tax, or investment advice.
- Draft-never-send for any outbound (partner note, landlord, etc.).
- One month at a time unless they ask for a longer horizon.
- Never use em-dashes.

## Steps

1. Confirm the month and take-home income (or ask them to paste it).
2. List planned outgo: fixed (housing, utilities, debt minimums), variable (food, transport), and goals (cushion, debt extra, giving, saving).
3. Assign leftovers until income − planned outgo = 0. If overspent on paper, cut or delay categories with them; do not invent income.
4. Show a plain table: category → amount → notes. Flag anything still unassigned or underwater.
5. Name one next action (e.g. move transfers, cut a line, schedule a money-date).

## Completion

Zero-based month plan (table) + one next action. Nothing stored unless they ask for a note without secrets.

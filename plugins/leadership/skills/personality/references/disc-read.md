# DISC plain read

Use this only after the configured source returns a profile. Quote the stored letters. Add the matching lines below. If the source stores two letters, include both lines and do not add a third. If it stores no letters, do not invent any.

- **D.** Be direct and brief. Lead with the point and the decision you need.
- **I.** Be warm and collaborative. Leave room for their energy and for ideas.
- **S.** Be steady, private, and low-pressure. Do not put them on the spot in front of the team.
- **C.** Be precise and evidence-first. Bring the specific example and the standard you are using.

Keep the read to those lines. Do not add motives, childhood stories, or a ranking of who is easier to manage.

## Missing

"Profile missing." is the whole personality result when the source has nothing for that person, the source cannot be read, or the stored value is empty. Do not borrow another person's profile. Do not score a transcript to manufacture letters.

`coaching-angles` and `one-on-one-prep` call this skill. They do not read `people.profiles` themselves.

# One-on-one brief

```
## One-on-one prep
Person: <name>
Window: <start> through <end>
Private draft. Nothing sent.

### Recent one-on-ones
- <date> (<source>): <one factual line>
(or "No recent one-on-ones in the history source.")

### Notable moment
<quote or specific behavior> (<source>, <timestamp>)
(or "No specific moment in this window.")

### Open commitments
They owe:
- <commitment> (due <date or "no date">)
Manager owes:
- <commitment>
Shared:
- <commitment>
(or "No open commitments.")

### Last feedback
<the commitment or the recorded feedback>
Acted on: <yes, no, unknown, or "not on the log">

### Growth area
<from the skill assessment, or "Growth area missing.">

### Topics to consider
1. <question or topic>
2. <question or topic>

### Delivery
<personality plain read, or "Profile missing.">
```

Topics are prompts for the manager, not lines to read aloud. Two to four is the cap. Tie at least one topic to an open commitment when one exists, and one to the growth area when it exists.

This is broader than a calendar list plus a task list: the moment, the last feedback, and the delivery note have to be earned from the history, the assessment, `open-items`, and `personality`. If a source is empty, leave that section empty and say so.

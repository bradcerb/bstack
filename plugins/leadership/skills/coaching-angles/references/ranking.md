# Ranking one moment

Keep a moment only when every line below is true. If one fails, skip the person.

- The behavior is specific: something this person did or said, not a trait and not a vibe.
- You can point at a quote or a timestamp in a transcript you read for this window.
- The skill assessment names a growth area for this person, and this moment is about that area.
- You can say the impact on the work in one or two sentences.
- One small next step is obvious. The person could do it before the next regular check-in.

When several moments qualify, keep the one that is most specific, most clearly tied to the growth area, and easiest to act on. Drop the rest. Do not summarize the dropped ones as extra feedback.

Secondhand reports, meetings outside the window, and moments you cannot source are out.

Say "no moment in this window" when you skip. Do not explain the near misses. A near miss list becomes a critique list.

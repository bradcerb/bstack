# Method notes

Short grounding for this plugin. Paraphrase only. Do not quote these sources at length, and do not paste book chapters into a brief.

## Bruce Tulgan

From *It's Okay to Be the Boss* and *How to Be the Boss*:

- Coach on a regular cadence, while the work is in motion, not only after a miss piles up.
- Tie the conversation to a concrete expectation: what good looks like for this person on this piece of work.
- Keep a record of what was agreed, and look at it again next time.
- Prefer a small course correction soon over a rare, heavy review.
- Use a structured one-on-one for the real work conversation. The manager still has that conversation in person.

## Wes Kao

From her writing on feedback that people can use:

- Name a specific behavior, not a vague trait.
- Tie that behavior to its impact on the work.
- Make one next step easy to act on.
- One clear point beats a list of critiques.

## How the skills use this

`coaching-angles` keeps a single observed moment and one next step. `one-on-one-prep` sets up a regular, specific conversation and refuses to script it. `open-items` is the commitment record the next conversation can check. `personality` only shapes delivery. `onboarding` only fills config.
